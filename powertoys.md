## Keyboard Manager

### Keys

- `Print Screen` to `Caps Lock`

- `Caps Lock` to `Backspace`

- `\` to `Shift (Left)`

### Shortcuts

- `Shift (Left)` + `Tab` to `Ctrl (Left)` + `Tab` (All Apps)

- `Ctrl (Left)` + `Tab` to `Alt (Left)` + `Tab` (All Apps)

- `Win (Left)` + `C` to `Alt (Left)` + `F4` (All Apps)

- `Win (Left)` + `T` starts `wt.exe --profile "Ubuntu"` (All Apps)
